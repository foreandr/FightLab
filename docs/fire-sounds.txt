Fire sound authoring

Fire immediately resolves to Flame whoosh unless a valid saved fire sound is present.
Confirming the flame style opens the inline Fire sound tier. The seven waveform cards
audition sound without casting or paying resources. Play [1] casts with
the pending choice; Confirm saves appearance.sound_id in the existing spell draft.
Back to Flame styles discards unconfirmed sound changes. Confirmed choices survive
style changes and re-entry. Buttons support keyboard focus and activation.

assets/spells/fire/sounds.gd owns seven original deterministic synthesized fire
Foley designs. No external audio licenses or downloads are needed. combat_audio.gd
caches their PCM streams once on demand. combat_runtime.gd resolves the chosen
sound at actual projectile release for every mode; physical projectiles retain dash.
Unknown IDs fall back to the default fire sound. Existing projectile sounds are not
stopped when a new visual style or sound is previewed.

Validation: tests/fire_sound_selection_test.gd exercises unique waveforms, default
routing, real Create selection/preview/confirmation/save, and actual release in a
bare new combat mode. tests/fire_style_gallery_test.gd checks the preceding tier.

Sustained burning

assets/spells/fire/burning.gd supplies one cached, seamless synthesized burn loop,
also exposed as Fire library.Burning. It is separate from the seven casting sounds.
scripts/burning_audio.gd creates source-owned AudioStreamPlayer3D emitters with
3 m unit size, 24 m maximum distance and capped volume. Native 3D attenuation and
panning use the active camera/listener. Each source shares the exact PCM resource.
CharacterBurnVisual follows the torso and stops on cleanse, death or burn expiry.
SpellGroundFire owns the same emitter on walls/floors and stops at patch expiry.
Removing/resetting either effect frees its emitter, avoiding orphan loops. The
shared audio mute is respected; cast/impact voice recycling cannot cut off a burn.
Burn damage timing, potency, duration and ticks are unchanged.
Validation: burning_audio_test.gd plus combat_presentation_contract_test.gd.

Gallery layout

Sound selection uses the same asset_preview_card.gd component as flame styles:
image, caption, theme, 6 px padding, selection outline and keyboard focus. The
sound image is an actual PCM waveform with a playback mark. Three/four columns
adapt to width. Images/streams are built and cached only when entering Sounds.
