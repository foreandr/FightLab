Sandbox practice controls (2026-11-03)

1 / Play: experimental held cast. The existing per-animation min/max duration
and shared cost/damage formulas still determine strength. At 1/3, 2/3 and full
hold, CastChargeFeedback strengthens the shared body-conforming barrier shell
and CombatAudio plays progressively higher cached charge tones. The visual
source is independent of actual shields, so charge neither grants shielding nor
clears a real barrier when it fades. Each threshold fires
once per hold. Release/cancel fades the overlay; feedback adds no damage bonus.
This actor-owned presentation component can be reused by other input adapters.

2: physical Left Cross (keyboard only; no hotbar button). The existing right
cross is baked through animation_mirror.gd by vendor/build_left_cross.gd. Its
derived source metadata is embedded in assets/animations/demos/left_cross.res. Runtime uses the
same begin_strike path, stamina, aiming, contact hitboxes, parries and reactions.
The old 4-key jab shortcut is removed from Spell Creation/Sandbox.
F1: sending/receiving hitboxes. F2: combat numbers. These observer hotkeys also
match CPU Free Fight; numeric 2/3 no longer toggle those debug overlays.

Left-click the human target to select it and open its behavior picker. Camera
focus remains independent. T is the keyboard route to that same menu.
Passive stops new attacks; an already-committed cast completes normally.
Repeat left punch closes to contact range and uses BattleActor.start_left_jab.
Repeat left-punch fireball uses Rules.author and start_authored_cast every 2s.
Fight me reuses CPU Duel Brain and CPU Duel Loadout, without separate combat math.
No target is spawned/replaced/healed on selection. Existing God Mode is respected.
Attacks stop when the target or player dies. Reset scene returns behavior to Passive.
Sandbox no longer auto-spawns the separate legacy Barrier test units; the selected
human and its behavior picker provide those practice roles.

Browsing now has Movement, Idle, Arms, Legs, Head, Air, Grabs and Crouch. Tabs
use two rows to stay within the sidebar. Crouch includes explicit crouch tags,
the built-in crouch idle/walk, and the existing authored low sweep/slide kick.
Body-region ownership types and combat rules are unchanged by these browse tags.

Jump-charge braking is softened from 14/s to 5/s. Initial motion coasts to rest
without new input reaccelerating it. Input adds a smoothly eased torso lean up to
6 degrees fore/aft and 5 degrees sideways; feet stay planted. WASD and arrows
share the same camera-relative direction. Stamina recovery stays blocked while
the jump charge is held and sprint adds no extra drain during the charge.

Checks: sandbox_jump_input_test, sandbox_action_tabs_test,
sandbox_practice_modes_test, held_cast_preview_test, practice_jab_test,
and variable_motion_test.

The pre-existing standalone practice_jab_test still fails its legacy Barrier
tester's CPU-contact setup, before reaching the player-jab assertion. This also
failed before the right-jab change. Its key/hand expectations now follow the new
binding. sandbox_practice_modes_test independently verifies real right-jab contact,
CPU left-punch contact, ranged hits, and death through the same combat runtime.
