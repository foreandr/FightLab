Burn death presentation

A fatal burn tick calls BattleActor.enter_death with cause="burn". Death creates
its normal single CombatRagdoll; BurnDeath reuses it. There is no second ragdoll
or second set of rigid bodies. Brief bounded impulses make the corpse writhe for
1.15 seconds, then a per-instance charred dissolve and grey flakes remove it over
1.25 seconds. A small ash mound follows the level's surface position and normal.

BurnDeath owns stepping this terminal corpse, so editor pose ownership and round
end cannot freeze the sequence. BattleActor.tick skips that owned corpse to avoid
double stepping. Normal physical deaths retain their existing ragdoll behavior.
God mode prevents burn death. Damageable practice humans can visibly burn to death.

Character burn flames and the existing shared burn loop continue through the
sequence, then fade/stop at ash completion. No extra damage ticks are introduced.
The dissolved body's ragdoll and receiving hitboxes are removed/disabled. Reset
restores original surface overrides, mesh visibility, hurtbox layers and skeleton,
and removes particles and ash. Source meshes/materials are never mutated.

Test: tests/burn_death_test.gd validates actual fatal burn, one ragdoll, round-end
and pose-locked progression, saved material isolation, ash and sound cleanup,
reset restoration, physical death and God mode. Shared presentation contract also
covers actual Story/Create and a bare new mode.
