Shared spell asset libraries

assets/spells/fire/
  library.gd  One Fire collection; selection(style_id, sound_id) produces saved settings.
  styles.gd   All 74 Fire styles and their source/artist/license metadata.
  sounds.gd   The seven Fire sounds and their default/synthesis definitions.

scripts/spell_asset_library.gd is the element registry. for_element("Fire") and
for_spell(spec) resolve the same library regardless of delivery_type. Projectile,
Touch, Beam, Barrier and future types reuse these IDs, catalogs and source assets.
No copies of styles or sounds belong inside delivery folders. The UI names both
steps Fire · Styles and Fire · Sounds so the collection remains recognizable.

Existing raw textures remain in the shared assets/combat/vfx directory, including
community_fire and external; catalog file references and original credit records
remain authoritative. Synthesized sounds are cached by shared combat_audio.gd.
Changing library organization does not duplicate textures/audio or load every PNG.

Save format remains appearance.fire_style_id and appearance.sound_id. Original
scripts/fire_style_catalog.gd and fire_sound_catalog.gd are compatibility wrappers,
not additional catalogs. Existing spells and internal tools keep their IDs.

Touch and other disabled deliveries remain disabled. This supplies shared asset
access and verifies it across delivery types without implementing those mechanics.
