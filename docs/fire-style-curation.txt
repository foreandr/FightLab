Fire gallery curation

The gallery offers 23 reviewed structures, not hundreds of combinations of
minor width, bend, turbulence, speed or colour changes.

Audit: all 533 previous presets were rendered through SpellAppearance at
0.0, 0.13, 0.29, 0.47, 0.71, 1.03, 1.47 and 2.13 seconds. Silhouette comparison
included temporal offsets so a shifted animation phase does not count as a
new design. Near-neighbour imported plumes, repeated mask variants, and
Cartesian combinations were removed from the visible choices. The retained
3D shapes differ in actual geometry, not just their projection in one image.
510 redundant or minor-variation choices are no longer offered.

All styles use the same red (#e62917) for both primary and accent, with the
same glow and opacity. Source texture RGB cannot tint fire. Transparency and
shape may vary; colour is not a selectable source of variety.

FireStyleCatalog.GALLERY_STYLES is the explicit visible shortlist. Legacy
IDs and original files remain readable for existing saved spells, but are
not advertised as additional gallery options. Do not reintroduce a Cartesian
product to inflate the count. New styles require structural/motion review.
