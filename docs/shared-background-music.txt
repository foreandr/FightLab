Menus and combat use /root/SharedMusic (background_music.gd).
Home enters a menu context; CombatWorld enters a combat context through its
inherited _enter_tree hook, including Story hosts that override _ready.
Hosts do not own players, duplicate playlists or restart music on menu subpages.

assets/audio/music/catalog.json is the single source of tracks and credits.
Only entries with status pool/active and an existing local loop are playable.
Candidate links remain metadata until permission and a compact audio loop exist.
Selections avoid the previous ID when alternatives exist. One-track pools keep
playing without restarting. Scene changes crossfade at equal power over 1.5 s;
loop seams are baked into the excerpts. Pauses and slow motion do not pitch-shift
or interrupt music. Music uses Master so global mute/volume applies everywhere.
