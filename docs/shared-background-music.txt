Menus and combat use /root/SharedMusic (background_music.gd).
Home enters a menu context; CombatWorld enters a combat context through its
inherited _enter_tree hook, including Story hosts that override _ready.
Hosts do not own players, duplicate playlists or restart music on menu subpages.

assets/audio/music/catalog.json is the single source of tracks and credits.
Only entries with status pool/active and an existing local loop are playable.
Candidate links remain metadata until permission and a compact audio loop exist.
Selections avoid the previous ID when alternatives exist. One-track pools keep
playing without restarting. Scene changes crossfade at equal power over 1.5 s;
loop seams are baked into the excerpts. Pauses and slow motion do not pitch-shift
or interrupt music. Music uses Master so global mute/volume applies everywhere.

Playable pool: 30 unique CC0 tracks, approximately 28.1 MB total. All full-length
source downloads were removed after the compact loops were created. The earlier
The Descent loop was removed when the user clarified that no special music
permissions are held. The Descent, Deadly Combat, Hill Giant, Dangerous Encounter
and Tense Fight remain reference candidates only; YouTube titles alone do not
establish free video-game use. No unverified candidate audio is included.

Validation: tests/main_menu_music_test.gd checks menu/combat pool identity,
looping streams, master bus, submenu continuity, no-repeat selection and persistent
playback across scenes. combat_runtime_contract_test.gd checks a new combat host.

CC0 batch (2026-11-02): 30 unique source tracks, 28.1 MB total.
Each loop is about 21–70 s, with 2 s tail/head overlap, brief codec-edge ramps,
and level adjustment with peak headroom. Full originals are not shipped.
Catalog entries retain source/download URLs, creator, license URL, source/audio
hashes, duration, edit start, overlap, seam measurement and file size.
CREDITS.txt lists all authors and source pages. music_pool_test.gd validates every
stream, all 30 licenses, uniqueness, compact size and loop-boundary measurements.
