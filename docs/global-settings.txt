Universal settings

Open Settings from Home > Options > Settings, or F9 in any mode.
The shared dialog pauses the scene while open and restores its previous pause
and mouse state when closed. The shared controls legend is under Home > Options.

Camera: closest and farthest orbit distance, in meters. Defaults preserve the
previous third-person limits (2.97 to 20.52). Limits are shared by Story, Create,
inspection, Take Control, and character placement. Moving either bound past the
other keeps the interval valid. Existing controller pitch/focus behavior stays
with the controller.

Audio: master volume, combat volume, mute, and distance affects volume.
Master affects every audio bus. Combat controls cast sounds and burn loops.
Turning distance volume off preserves spatial panning but removes distance
attenuation/culling from those combat voices, including active burn loops.
Cast timing, native pitch, burn rules, and spell balance are unchanged.

All values persist in user://global_settings.cfg, independent of saved spells
and characters. GameSettings is a project autoload. Reusable components resolve
it at runtime through game_settings_access.gd so standalone SceneTree scripts
can preload the same mechanics before autoloads are initialized. Future modes
use the same service and dialog rather than adding their own preferences.

Validation: tests/global_settings_test.gd covers persistence, camera limits,
shared Create/Story dialog, pause/resume, audio buses and live distance changes.
The spell audio timing regression also passes.
