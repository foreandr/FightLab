SHARED CHARACTER MECHANICS

Combat consolidation

  See docs/combat-runtime.txt for the new-mode API and integration example.
  CombatWorld is the reusable gameplay base; CombatRuntime owns actual casting,
  projectile flight, impacts, actor/wall updates, beams and pulses. SpellBuilder
  uses combat_spell_rules.gd to construct/preview specs and the same runtime to
  commit casts. melee_contact.gd owns swept-limb resolution across actor adapters.
  Story/Create share these implementations. The old visual-only SpellArena study
  is now a sibling, not the parent of gameplay arenas.

Level independence

  See docs/combat-levels.txt. CombatLevel provides optional bounds, local spawns
  and terrain queries through CombatWorld.use_level. The pit is one provider;
  generated levels use the same interface. An unspecified level is unbounded.
  Actor movement/recovery and editor placement must not assume world zero,
  pit dimensions or a flat floor. Environmental patches retain terrain height.

Ownership

scripts/combat_hit_regions.gd
  Shared physical hit-region rules and presentation names/colors. Head hits apply
  2x direct physical damage; every other receiving region stays at 1x. Runtime
  applies this once after shield absorption, before actor health handling.
  Spell specs, status potency/duration, burn ticks, resource costs and knockback
  are not multiplied. DuelActor selects existing Hit_Head / Hit_Chest clips with
  smooth playback and recovery. Strong/lethal hits retain their ragdoll behavior.
  Hitboxes [2]: orange head, pink body, no floating region captions. Only
  the struck collision shape flashes yellow/fills for 0.45 seconds. Debug meshes
  never change collision; disabling/resetting clears highlights.

scripts/combat_defense_rules.gd
  Provisional nonlethal ragdoll threshold: 40 actual physical damage from a hit.
  Headshot doubling counts toward it; impulse still drives knockback and ragdoll
  motion, but high impulse or a launch flag alone no longer triggers hit ragdoll.
  Death still ragdolls. Armor is cosmetic: no damage/impulse absorption; legacy
  armor spells no longer grant protection. Compatibility armor fields remain for
  old content/tools. The authoring panel predicts unblocked body/head outcomes
  using these same rules; lethal hits and target-specific reductions are caveats.
  Barrier/block redesign and a permanently blocking test dummy are deferred.

scripts/combat_head_severance.gd
  Lethal physical head hits detach humanoid heads through the shared impact path.
  Split corpse-local skinned mesh indices/weights at the head boundary, retaining
  surface materials, hair and head accessories. Release the existing ragdoll neck
  joint and launch that existing head body; no extra rigid bodies are created.
  Sources remain immutable and reset restores original meshes. Nonlethal head
  hits, body kills, burn deaths, God Mode and wooden practice dummies do not sever.
  Also supports a lethal head hit on an already-ragdolled character.

Guarded sideways strafe authoring
  scripts/vendor/copy_strafe_guard.gd copies both arm/finger channels from
  duel/walk_back into strafe_left/right via animation_channels.replace_channels.
  The saved clips keep their original leg/root tracks and looping timing. The
  full duel generator repeats the same operation so rebuilding preserves it.
  Backward walking and both strafes share a subtle opposing arm sway: shoulders
  swing +/-8 degrees, elbows counter by 4 degrees. The tool preserves the clean
  arm source in metadata so regeneration never compounds this edit.
  Story's strafe arm layers use these same clips rather than a separate guard.
  Run the copy tool with Godot --headless --path . --script
  res://scripts/vendor/copy_strafe_guard.gd to reproduce the edit.

scripts/combat_floating_numbers.gd

  CharacterStatusVisual is installed by BattleActor, observes all status fields,
  and owns burn/frozen-leg attachments plus stun/chill/haste motes. It ignores
  mode/editor flags. ElementalVFX owns the particle animation/expiry clock; modes
  must not call vfx.step. The same presentation contract runs on actual Story,
  actual Create and a bare CombatWorld; see combat-presentation-audit.txt.

  Shared fire presentation: fire_plume.gd/fire_plume.gdshader draw animated,
  upright tongues with capped CPU embers/smoke. spell_ground_fire.gd owns the
  stationary scorch patch; spell_environment drives its final 1.2s flame fade.
  character_burn_visual.gd follows six torso/limb bone anchors on BattleActor,
  created lazily on burn, fading on expiry/death and cleared on reset. Story's
  duplicate chest-puff rendering is removed. Presentation does not set status,
  damage, potency, costs or patch lifetime. tests/fire_visuals_test.gd verifies
  Compatibility rendering, moving anchors, limits, extinction and reset.

  Burn ticks use the same Numbers [3] display: red, typed burn amounts every
  half-second, from actual applied damage including God-mode practice counters.
  battle_actor.update_status emits them for all modes. Turning numbers off
  suppresses presentation only. tests/burn_floating_numbers_test.gd checks ticks,
  credit, color, God-mode counters and lethal damage capping.
  Optional per-world diagnostic presentation, enabled by set_combat_numbers.
  Costs come from actual committed actor spending, physical hits from received
  damage counters. Do not substitute RHS estimates or health loss on immortal
  targets. Labels rise/fade, follow actors, respect actor visibility and have a
  bounded lifetime/count. Spell Numbers [3] is a mode input adapter to this API.
  Successful physical head hits display larger gold HEADSHOT <amount> text.
  Keep the underlying event physical; do not emit a second ordinary damage label.

scripts/physical_momentum.gd
  Physical damage uses actual horizontal speed in any direction, not sprint
  flags or a character speed stat. Melee adds 3 percent per horizontal m/s. Older
  limb-speed calculations subtract attacker root velocity before their existing
  animation-speed multiplier, keeping root movement in the shared bonus.
  Projectiles inherit 35 percent of horizontal speed along their existing aim
  direction and physical damage scales by actual launch speed / nominal speed.
  Effects, size and resource costs do not gain that bonus. Capture once at launch;
  later caster motion cannot alter an in-flight shot. Both arena modes share the
  rules, and Create's RHS previews the same calculation live while moving.
  Unrestricted preview fields remain independent. Prediction and actual spawning
  use the same BattleArena.projectile_launch_state.

Neutral spell physical damage
  combat_spell_rules.gd calibrates damage speed against the full-power, one-second,
  50/50 size/speed, centered-timing setup: 8.35 m/s base x 1.125 = 9.39375 m/s.
  A neutral offensive elementless cast therefore costs 10 mana and 10 stamina,
  and applies 10 physical damage before defenses. Projectile size/speed and the
  existing costs are unchanged. Damage factors shown in the RHS are relative to
  this neutral speed and amp; unrestricted test damage remains independent.

scripts/projectile_allocation.gd
  normalize/change preserve the size/speed/effect mix. spend applies Total power
  (0..1) without reallocating unused power. Spell UI shows actual spent shares,
  with each channel limited by that selected budget. Full power preserves old
  behavior; zero uses minimum projectile size/speed and no effect. Total power
  is saved with the spell and older drafts default to 100 percent. Limiter-off
  controls remain independent, hiding the balanced budget row until restored.
  This controls allocation, not the existing animation/resource-cost formulas.
  Entering the projectile effect stage always starts elementless with 100 percent
  Total power, a 50/50 size/speed split, a one-second demo cast, centered timing (30 percent release within
  the 5..55 range), and Limiter on. Changes are retained while editing that stage;
  returning through delivery starts with these entry defaults again.

scripts/character_hitbox_overlays.gd
  Field.set_combat_hitboxes toggles sending projectile spheres (cyan) and receiving
  actor collision shapes (pink) together. Spell Hitboxes [2] uses this shared API;
  set_projectile_hitboxes remains a compatibility adapter. Receiving outlines use
  exact capsule dimensions as wireframes, follow CollisionShape3D transforms and update on shape
  changes. Include active wooden-dummy shapes, hide inactive shapes, and never
  change collision layers or gameplay shapes. No mesh rebuilding while disabled.

scripts/projectile_area_impact.gd
  Shared live projectile impact area for Story and Create. Splash reaches twice
  the projectile radius around first contact (or world impact), with one normal
  apply_impact call per enemy, independent of the number of overlapping hurtboxes.
  Uses each target's normal defenses/status handling; excludes the caster/team,
  respects solid cover, and does not add piercing or repeated flight damage.
  The collision query includes enough results for all registered fighter areas.

scripts/spell_test_overrides.gd
  Limiter-off values are session-only final overrides: duration/release, size,
  speed, effect, mana, stamina and physical damage. Changing one never changes
  another. Percent controls use normal maximum size/speed/effect as 100 percent.
  Specs are marked unrestricted_preview and testing_only. BasicProjectile resolves
  their final geometry/speed without gameplay clamps (zero radius uses a tiny
  positive physics representation). SpellBuilder.save_draft rejects limiter-off
  state; raw values never enter preferences. Re-enabling restores balanced inputs.

scripts/duel_actor.gd and scripts/battle_actor.gd
  God Mode is a per-actor practice_god_mode flag. Shared resource spending and
  recovery honor unlimited stamina/mana; BattleActor preserves full health while
  retaining successful hit events, damage accounting, guard and status reactions.
  Spell practice edits only the selected character and saves independent caster,
  dummy and human choices. Reset preserves these choices. No selection disables
  the toggle rather than silently targeting another character.

scripts/combat_aim.gd
  Cursor rays, focused chest anchors, local aim angles and front-hemisphere limits.
  Live spells use ray_point. Authored Story calibration uses calibration_point;
  its near-plane limits intentionally preserve existing saved aim maps.
  Free projectile aim passes fighters and radius to ray_point. Near silhouettes
  choose a convergence depth at the front of their hurt bounds, preserving the
  exact cursor ray rather than aiming toward a fighter's center or distant wall.
  Camera position and lock-on targeting remain independent.

scripts/character_aim_pose.gd
  Arm-first corrections, torso dead zones, hip participation, leg yaw and hand
  endpoint alignment. Call after sampling the base pose and before ice constraints.
  Story's existing recovery controller delegates to these functions. Spell preview
  passes an optional aim dictionary through SpellCastMotion.pose.

scripts/character_movement.gd
  Camera-relative movement, flat direction, target-facing and root turning.
  Story, Create and Take Control keep their input adapters and use these rules.
  directional_speed owns angle-based speed factors. Forward / forward diagonal /
  side / backward diagonal / backward: walk 100/90/80/70/60 percent; sprint
  100/90/75/60/50 percent of each controller's forward base speed. Analog angles
  interpolate smoothly. Buffs still scale the result; diagonals are normalized.
  Applies to ordinary controlled locomotion, not authored jumps/dashes/knockback.
  gait_speed shares stamina tiers: below 40 no sprint; below 10 forced walking
  at half the controller's normal run pace, with a walking pose. Existing speed
  buffs still apply. God Mode bypasses these limits.

scripts/character_exertion.gd
  actor.use_sprint(delta, requested, moving) is the common sprint cost/gating API.
  Create, Take Control (keyboard/gamepad) and Story spend 12 stamina per second,
  with a 0.4-second recovery delay. Idle recovery cannot cancel ongoing drain.
  Stationary Shift is free; walking retains ordinary recovery. God Mode bypasses
  cost and gait limits. Sprint drain stops at 40 stamina; reaching the threshold
  falls back to running (walking below 10) and requires releasing sprint before
  trying again after recovery, avoiding alternating walk/sprint every few frames.
  Continuous drain updates resource bookkeeping without spawning per-frame cost
  labels. Reset clears exhaustion. New movement controllers should call this API.

scripts/third_person_camera.gd
  Camera adoption, lock/release, yaw calculation, orbit, zoom and follow framing.
  Story and Create use the same state operations. Take Control shares framing,
  retaining its gamepad/keyboard shortcuts and restoring the authoring camera.

scripts/combat_pointer.gd
  Mouse-following pointer placement and mouse-input transparency. Story's cursor
  and Create's crosshair retain their drawings and inherit this common base.
  dialog_open identifies visible embedded windows. Spell free aim yields to
  dialogs: show the system cursor, hide the crosshair and suspend orbit/input;
  normal arena pointer behavior resumes after dismissal.

scripts/projectile_impact_predictor.gd
  Read-only forecast from the actual casting origin. Uses the same enemy sphere
  radius, shield checks and world collision policy as live flight. The field API
  predict_projectile_impact is reusable by modes with a known projectile spec.
  BasicProjectile.resolved_motion, lifetime and the field's projectile_aim_target
  are shared by spawning and prediction. This diagnostic API is read-only;
  the rejected amber impact overlay is no longer displayed in Create.

scripts/animation_channels.gd
  Channel filtering and bounded read-only filtered-clip cache (128 entries).
  Repeated consumers of a source Animation reuse its filtered resource. Editing
  a source invalidates cached variants; clips already playing remain snapshots.
  Each actor owns its AnimationPlayers and lightweight library bindings, never
  shared playback state. Consumers must not edit cached clips in place.

Contracts

  Selection is independent from lock-on. Hover is blue; click selects gold.
  Clicking empty ground clears selection without releasing an active lock.
  F locks/replaces/releases focus; release preserves zoom and orbit.
  Existing Story calibration method names remain compatibility adapters.
  Native authoring, scrubbing and locomotion do not receive aim corrections unless
  an aim request is explicitly passed to the animation sampler.
  Authored costs/damage/allocation live in combat_spell_rules.gd; mana scaling
  stays in animation_mana_scaling.gd. DuelActor owns atomic payment/recovery.
  Runtime execution never repeats authoring or charges a second resource cost.
  This consolidation preserves the neutral benchmark and existing balance curves.

Adding a mode

  Start with CombatWorld; see docs/combat-runtime.txt. Supply input, controlled
  actor, selected target, active camera and sampled pose.
  Reuse these modules; do not copy Story or Create mechanics into the new screen.
  Keep mode-specific UI, saved preferences, calibration data and animation clocks
  outside the shared math. Use existing animation resources rather than rebuilding
  filtered clips, meshes or libraries every frame.

Cast eligibility: CombatRuntime.can_start_cast gates animation on the authored stamina cost (God Mode bypass). Mana is checked at release; a failed release displays Not enough mana above the actor while the animation continues and attempted stamina is charged. Resource notices are independent of the experimental number toggle.

Spell demo locomotion: animation_cast_type.gd owns animation_type (Head, Arm, Leg, Movement). Head affects the head/neck only; Arm affects torso/clavicles/arms. Both retain locomotion outside their region. Leg affects pelvis/legs, retaining an idle upper-body baseline; Movement retains the full pose, suspend sprint acceleration/drain until recovery, and coast along the initial world-space horizontal velocity. Exponential drag is 4.5/s for Leg and 2.25/s for Movement; below 0.05 m/s drift settles to zero. Steering/speed and the locomotion pose return smoothly from 0% onward for Leg (uncapped), or during the final quarter for Movement (at most 0.35 s), after spell release; vertical motion/gravity stay with their existing providers. CreationLocomotion.cast_legs consumes the type, not an arbitrary blend percentage. Jump Dismantle is Movement with an Arm layer; knee/roundhouse are Leg.

Spell demos also include Right Roundhouse from the existing attack catalog and Jump Dismantle baked at 60 Hz from foreandr's saved JUMP DISMANTLE timeline. The portable demo resource contains skeletal jump elevation; its adjacent JSON retains the source timeline and credits. No momentum/slowdown mechanic was added.

Animation authors must declare Animation type in the custom editor Movement tab. Stored in custom JSON, variants and undo/redo. New Store imports require choosing a type before adding an asset; the scripting StoreLibrary.take API rejects undeclared types. Existing owned/custom entries migrate using recorded limb/category metadata, with unknown entries conservatively Whole body. Built-in catalog entries explicitly declare types. Spell demo preview type overrides persist separately from spell effect definitions. Authored specs carry the active type into CombatSpellRules: Leg casts receive a tentative 1.20 physical multiplier before shared projectile/movement scaling, applied once. Mana, stamina, spell amp and burn scaling are unchanged. Physical calculation windows show this multiplier. Existing committed casts retain their captured animation settings.

Cast performance: CombatSpec.copy isolates mutable gameplay fields while sharing AssetProvenance.snapshot graphs. Snapshot graphs recursively freeze every parent/citation once per loaded or newly authored revision; no history is dropped. Unfrozen legacy inputs retain deep-copy isolation. Spell demo sources are resolved/cached on workspace entry and refreshed on re-entry so casts and demo clicks do not rescan folders.

Typed composition: each animation contribution declares Head, Arm, Leg or Movement. Old Arms/Legs/Whole body declarations normalize to Arm/Leg/Movement. Regional contributions on EVERYTHING rows participate only in their own channels, so an Arm overlay does not erase the Movement base. Higher priority still wins on overlapping regions. Composite library saves retain animation_components plus the original composed_timeline; a component extracted to a specific track records that regional contribution as well as its source type. Overall composite type derives from contributions, with Movement taking precedence. Dismantle retains Movement + Arm. Custom variants preserve components and source animation_resource references through save/load and undo/redo.

Demo type visibility: every demo row shows its effective declared type, including live preview overrides and Movement + Arm for Dismantle. Headbutt supplies a Head example with only neck/head keyed rotation tracks; Arm, Leg and Movement examples remain available. Eight demos cover all four types.

Demo duration continuity: selecting a preset or collection animation uses the chosen demo_duration as its playback length while retaining source_duration for source sampling. Confirming Projectile preserves this length and the timeline view span. The initial default remains one second; only an explicit duration edit changes it. Playback restarts at zero for a newly selected demo.

Cast recovery: AnimationCastType.recovery_weight supplies one shared smoothstep weight for both world velocity and pose. While it is zero, existing cast drift/braking remain unchanged. During recovery, drift blends toward current requested movement and the cast blends toward the current gait; sprint eligibility/cost still use CharacterExertion. Released casts stay committed through their end. Completed full recovery skips the redundant post-cast fade; regional Head/Arm exit fades leave locomotion outside their region running normally.

Leg recovery starts at 0% of the authored duration and spans the full animation, without a seconds cap; Movement keeps its existing last-quarter/0.35s cap. Both still wait for spell release if it is later.

Animation pose safety: character_pose_safety.gd owns the distributed 90-degree local spine budget used by direct rig sampling, timeline composition, SpellCastMotion and BattleActor playback, including authored frames; root/pelvis acrobatics remain intact. CharacterAimPose uses facing-based torso aim (not the swinging hand), bounded forearm corrections that fade for rearward targets, and shared capsule-proxy overlap checks from pose_self_collision.gd. Unsafe aim is reduced toward its native pose instead of forcing an unreachable hand direction. Projection is stateless for repeatable backwards scrubbing. These are conservative bone-volume safeguards, not exact mesh-versus-mesh collision or a guarantee that every imported source pose is penetration-free. Existing source overlaps are not allowed to worsen under the aim correction.

Regional combat aim: CharacterAimPose.direction_angles is the common facing-based yaw/pitch calculation for hand, elbow, knee, foot and head contacts. Legs steer at the thigh/hip without independently twisting the calf; elbows steer at the upper arm; head contacts distribute correction over neck and head. Each region uses anatomical angle bounds and the same pose-safety projection, with explicit affected body volumes. Story's leg/head adapter calls the same apply function as spell preview rather than adding its own torso or calf-yaw layer. Contact points, hitboxes and projectile origins continue following the resulting bones. Animation types, movement recovery, costs and punch tuning are preserved.

Spell wizard controls: SpellPlayback is visible from the initial demo step through type/effect editing; side panels reserve its bottom space. An explicit animation selection enables Play/Repeat and demo Confirm. Play [1], Repeat and Slow mo work before spell-type selection; 2/3 match the visible debug controls throughout. Type clicks set preview_delivery_type and use the existing projectile cast path without advancing/saving the type; ConfirmSpellType commits it and advances, retaining the preview's duration/timing/settings. A fresh visit to the type chooser requires another type preview. Leaving spell creation hides controls and restores playback speed.

Delivery mana pricing: CombatSpellRules.DELIVERY_MANA_MULTIPLIERS is the single table: Projectile 1.0, Beam 1.0, Targeted 1.25, Touch 0.5, Barrier 0.5. These provisional prices multiply the base/effect/stamina-transfer subtotal, then the existing release-time cost multiplier; stamina, physical damage, burn and motion are unchanged. Authored specs mark delivery_mana_priced to avoid double charging on load/execution; raw typed specs are priced by the same mana_cost API. The chooser shows percentages and defaults to a Projectile preview while still requiring Confirm. Non-projectile types remain disabled pending their implementation. A physical-only Touch punch is the intended contact-driven ability model; this change establishes pricing, not a new Touch dispatch path.

Touch contact contract — specified for future implementation:
- A Touch ability may declare one or more explicit contact regions and their sides when applicable: left/right hand, arm/elbow, leg/foot, knee, or head. Selecting an animation alone must not authorize contact from the whole body.
- Resolve contact from the actual bone-following collision volume of each chosen region. Unselected limbs and body regions cannot trigger the ability, including during large/full-body animations. A modest, consistently applied margin may enlarge only the selected regions' hitboxes for forgiveness; it must not become a whole-character proximity test.
- Reuse the shared swept-contact and combat-impact path so fast motion between frames remains detectable and behavior is consistent across modes/levels. Preserve the explicit region/side in saved ability metadata.
- Touch may carry physical damage alone, supporting the intended punch model. Touch casting remains disabled until its mechanics are implemented; this entry records the required behavior without enabling it.

Touch multi-contact stamina: provisional +25% of the animation's original stamina cost per additional distinct selected region. One/two/three regions on a 10-stamina animation cost 10/12.5/15 before other stamina adjustments. Shared CombatSpellRules reads touch_contacts as canonical bone IDs, deduplicates contacts (including toe/foot and head aliases), and adds the surcharge after effect-to-mana transfer. The extra contact cost stays in stamina; it does not raise mana, damage, burn or spell amp. Touch remains disabled; selection/validation and multi-region contact execution are future work.
