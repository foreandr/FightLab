Space in Sandbox uses BattleActor.start_motion_jump(1.0, true), release uses
set_motion_jump_held(false). Tap costs 4 stamina; full charge costs 20. Holding Space charges on the floor
over 0.85 seconds, progressively blending standing into ual1/Crouch_Idle. At
maximum the pose and payment stop increasing. Release launches with speed
5.4..15.6 m/s based on charge (about 0.6..5.4m measured height). Release no longer
cuts velocity in the air. Running out of stamina releases the affordable charge. Repeated presses while airborne or in
takeoff cannot start another jump. Resource checks and cast commitment still apply.

Stamina cannot regenerate while a jump charge is held, even at maximum charge;
release restores ordinary recovery eligibility. Mana recovery is unchanged.
CharacterMovement brakes carried horizontal speed exponentially (5/s) during
the charge and ignores new movement requests until release; input instead eases a small
6-degree fore/aft or 5-degree sideways torso lean. Sprint drain is
disabled during that charge. Both CombatWorld editor movement and live actor
movement use the same brake, preserving normal behavior outside the charge.

BattleActor's pose-locked path now advances the existing takeoff/air clocks.
CombatWorld still owns movement/gravity/collision; no editor-specific trajectory.
CreationLocomotion samples the actor's saved jump poses (or the existing default
takeoff/air animations). Arm/head casts can keep their upper body while the jump
supplies legs. Full-body Touch ownership remains intact. Entry/landing use the
existing preview pose blend.

Matrix classified as Movement describes its whole-body source, not a blanket
movement lock. AnimationCastType.applies currently enables type restrictions only
for Touch spells; Projectile/Barrier and unpaired Sandbox previews allow movement.
That rule is unchanged. Air and Grabs are now browsing sections, separate from
authored body-region types. Air collects airborne moves; Grabs collects grabs,
clinches and grappling. Airborne grabs appear in both. The Ground/Air radio row
is removed. No new Grab combat mechanics were introduced.

Validation: tests/sandbox_jump_input_test.gd exercises real Space input, progressive
crouch depth, stationary/max charge, capped payment and pose, release, tap/hold
height and stamina, grounded/airborne states, casting during a jump, regional pose
preservation, duplicate/insufficient-stamina rejection and unchanged Touch rules.
