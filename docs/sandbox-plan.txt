Sandbox mode (planned)

A separate mode alongside Story and Free Fight for unrestricted experiments.
God Mode belongs there, not in Create > Spell. Keep the shared actor-level
unlimited-resource capability reusable; do not duplicate combat mechanics.
Spell creation exposes no God Mode control, defaults to finite resources,
and ignores old God Mode preferences. Reset Scene still heals/restores targets.
Sandbox entry and mechanics have not been implemented yet.
