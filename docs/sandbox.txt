Sandbox uses sandbox_screen.gd over the standalone spell screen/world. It shares
the normal combat runtime, aiming, focus, resources, reactions and spell tools.
Its only target is a human; human_only_targets suppresses the mannequin visual.
Normal spell creation retains its original three characters and demo choices.
Sandbox drafts/preferences use separate files so arbitrary source animations and
testing choices do not leak into Create Spell's smaller demo-selection flow.

The first Sandbox step is sandbox_animation_browser.gd: custom/owned animations,
built-in clips, and the local community catalog, grouped in that priority order.
Browse by Arm/Leg/Head/Movement, source, airborne/grounded, names, tags or author.
Twelve cards per page; selection previews, Confirm advances. Existing duration
and playback controls are shared, not reimplemented. Metadata is loaded first;
full community records and animation resources are resolved only on selection
through the existing Store index/backend. Static/invalid/blocked Store records
remain excluded. Type icons identify cards; they are not fake pose thumbnails.
Own/built-in cards are usable while the community index opens in the background.
Search is debounced; page turns reuse matches. A bounded grid scroll protects
Confirm and pagination at small window sizes. Worker completion is joined once
when leaving, including leaving before catalog initialization finishes.

God mode remains per selected character (button/G), as in the shared actor API.
Tests: sandbox_animation_library_test checks the full catalog, all four types,
search/pagination, lazy source resolution, real non-demo and community motion,
preview/confirm separation, preserved duration, human target and reset.
free_fight_sandbox_menu_test covers menu entry/exit and God mode isolation.
standalone_spell_test is a graphical regression for the unchanged Create flow.
