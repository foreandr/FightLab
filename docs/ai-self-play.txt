PERSISTENT AI SELF-PLAY

Visible fights
--------------
Home -> Fight -> 1v1 CPU Demo learns by default. Each completed match updates and
saves the shared learner. Press T / Self-play to keep rematching automatically;
press it again to stop. Space pauses simulation. R rematches. The top commentary
panel has been removed. Resources remain above each character. Match report
contains decisions, completed learning matches, update count and champion version.

Offline batches
---------------
Close the visible CPU demo before using the same learning profile in a trainer.
From the FightLab folder, PowerShell:

  .\tools\train_ai.ps1 -Matches 1000 -Speed 4

This runs real Godot combat without a game window. It resumes the saved learner,
saves after each completed training match, and can be stopped between matches.
An interrupted unfinished match is not counted as a completed episode. Speed is
a requested acceleration, not a promised throughput. Physics frequency increases
with time scale so each simulated physics step stays 1/60 second; it does not
skip animation/contact frames to manufacture faster fights. CPU throughput may
limit actual acceleration.

Advanced direct entry:

  .\tools\Godot_v4.6-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path . --script res://scripts/ai/train_self_play.gd -- --matches=1000 --speed=4 --eval-every=100 --eval-pairs=10 --history-every=3 --seed=731091

--profile=user://ai/other_profile.json uses a separate model file.
--timeout=180 limits simulated seconds per training/evaluation game; a timeout is
a draw, not an invented win. Visible ordinary fights keep their real death-based
ending. Do not run concurrent writers on one profile. The revision check refuses
stale overwrites; separate worker profiles are not automatically merged.

What learns
-----------
The AI module is scripts/ai/, separate from modes, levels and combat mechanics.
AdaptivePolicy uses tabular semi-Markov Q-learning, not a neural network. Its
state buckets cover distance, both fighters' health, stamina, mana, enemy casting
and enemy shield. Its legal actions include the equipped melee/fireball choices,
combo links/stopping, and approach/sprint/dash/circle/retreat. Resource and range
masks, expert recovery and the existing precise block/evasion reflex remain in
the controller. This is a bounded tactical learner, not arbitrary motor control.

Reward per transition:
  +0.1 * damage dealt
  -0.1 * damage taken
  -0.003 * actual mana spent
  -0.002 * actual stamina spent
  -0.015 * elapsed simulated seconds
Terminal reward: +20 win, -20 loss, -5 draw.
Bootstrap discount: 0.97 per simulated second. Initial learning rate: 0.12,
decreasing with visits. Existing heuristic scores initialize unknown choices.
Live exploration is 6%; batch exploration decays from 18% to a 4% floor. Frozen
evaluation has zero exploration and cannot update its policy.

CombatWorld, BattleActor, CombatRuntime and the existing authored spell rules
still own animation clocks, real limb contacts/projectiles, damage, resource
payment/recovery, shields/parries, status ticks, ragdolls and death. Learning never
raises stats, widens parry windows or grants hidden resources. Levels remain
independent providers of geometry. Other modes can reuse AdaptivePolicy and
SelfPlayStore.best_model without duplicating combat.

Checkpoints and evidence
------------------------
Most batch games are self-play against the current learner. Every third training
game uses a frozen champion/historical opponent, alternating the learner's side,
to reduce pure self-play overfitting. Every 100 training games by default, the
runner freezes a candidate and evaluates both orientations on held-out seeds
against the incumbent, the untrained policy and archived champions.

Promotion requires at least 20 evaluation games, at least 10 against the incumbent,
70% aggregate points and 60% incumbent points. A draw earns half a point. These are
practical checkpoint gates, not statistical proof of superiority. The current
champion and up to five older policies are retained. A candidate that fails does
not replace the champion. Training still continues in the separate learner.

Every completed match supplies experience; it does NOT guarantee a stronger AI
on every update. Evaluation can expose regression, cyclic counters or reward
exploits. Thousands of matches and substantially broader evaluation are needed
before claiming general or superhuman strength. Changes to combat rules/loadouts
or state semantics should bump SelfPlayStore.RULESET and use a new profile.

Local files (not game/character content or Git assets)
----------------------------------------------------
Default: user://ai/self_play.json, plus .bak backup and temporary atomic save.
Windows: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\FightLab\ai\self_play.json
The profile contains learner values/visits, match/update counters, frozen champion,
small historical league and bounded evaluation/match histories. Corrupt profiles
can recover from a valid backup; unknown formats are preserved and not overwritten.
Batch report: <profile basename>_report.json, with seeds, outcomes, updates,
evaluation points and wall time. Normal match details remain in
user://logs/cpu_demo_last_match.json. One process should write a profile at a time.

Validation
----------
tests/ai_learning_test.gd: rewards change subsequent choices, legal masks,
read-only evaluation, persistence/reload/backups, incompatible-file preservation,
stale writer rejection and evidence-gated champion promotion.
tests/ai_self_play_ui_test.gd: no top panel, learner/opponent training flags,
completed-match persistence, automatic rematch, retained policy and stop toggle.
Headless smoke batches validate real fights, saving, resume and frozen evaluation.
They validate the pipeline; they are not a thousand-match strength benchmark.

Expanded CPU repertoire
-----------------------
Both fighters equip 54 authored choices across 36 existing animation clips:
42 Touch choices (physical and seven fire-enhanced contacts), 11 fireball choices,
and the timed shield. The original twelve melee timing variants, three fireballs
and their action IDs are retained; new choices have stable descriptive IDs.
Hooks, uppercuts, elbows, headbutts, slashes, sweeps and spinning kicks use explicit
contact regions. Flame contacts use the same allocation, mana, burn and hit rules
as player-authored Touch spells. Different fireball gestures remain ordinary
projectiles, not new delivery mechanics. Costs, combos, parries and movement
remain owned by the shared actor/runtime; no special damage or style buffs.
Headbutt is registered once per actor; contact geometry is warmed at setup.
The learned profile is preserved. New legal actions enter policy exploration
without erasing prior learning. This expands the repertoire; it does not establish
that the expanded policy is already stronger than the previous policy.
Validation: tests/cpu_repertoire_test.gd checks both actors can start, visibly
animate, release and pay for every choice; cpu_duel_test.gd runs three full fights.

Spectator spell labels: Spells [4] on the bottom toolbar toggles each fighter's
last successfully released spell name for 2.5 simulation seconds. Unreleased or
failed casts are not labelled; rematch clears old names. The compact toolbar
wraps into extra rows when needed rather than running outside the window.

CPU spectator result framing
---------------------------
Camera eye and look point use matching exponential smoothing. At finish_round,
cache the match midpoint, separation and each fighter's focus point; use those
stable anchors until rematch. Settling corpse physics and residual movement no
longer reframe the arena after the result. Orbit, zoom and selecting another
fighter remain available. Rematch clears the cached anchors. No combat or
ragdoll physics are disabled by this presentation fix.
tests/cpu_result_camera_test.gd exercises a real lethal hit/ragdoll, live target
smoothing, uneven frame times and settling movement, result stability, camera
controls after death, and rematch reset.

Touch chain balance and selection
--------------------------------
The shared touch_combo_rules.gd defines a 55% follow-up gate. Alternating a
left/right contact gives the next linked Touch animation 1.12x cast speed;
same-side links are legal at 1.0x. Head contacts get no side bonus. It does not
discount costs, alter damage, stack across links, or carry into separate casts.
Parry haste multiplies normally. Every BattleActor.chain_touch_cast caller uses
the same rule. Released projectile commitment is unchanged.
CPU choices favor compact opposite-hand follow-ups and retry unavailable range
or budget checks until 90% of the outgoing animation. Burn scoring counts extra
damage using Burn rules, discounting pending burn rather than assuming stacking.
Fire jab and cross join the existing flame hooks, elbow, kick and knee.
Validation: touch_combo_test.gd checks speed/costs/same-side/projectile exclusion;
cpu_combo_choices_test.gd checks burn scoring, range retry and a real physical
jab into fire cross. Three full fights passed; the last fixture recorded five
fire Touch casts and nine combo links. Perfect-block timing tests also passed.
