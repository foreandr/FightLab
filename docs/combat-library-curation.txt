Combat library cleanup — 2026-11-03

The shared Store index and Sandbox picker now accept combat actions, defensive
actions, weapons, spell gestures, acrobatics, locomotion and combat recovery.
data/combat_animation_keywords.json contains 2,557 word-aware terms and phrases.
The generator is scripts/vendor/build_combat_vocabulary.gd. It combines curated
action names, spelling/inflection variants and directional action phrases.
Generic fragments such as “forward”, “head”, “arm” and “cross” do not qualify
on their own. Names are tokenized with camel-case support. Full source
descriptions/filenames are considered, while inherited generic tags/categories
are not sufficient evidence. Compact Store rows retain the original evidence.

New source: Motifect Martial Arts Motion Pack
https://motifect.itch.io/motifect-martial-arts-motion-pack
40 original BVH downloads; 38 accepted, two essentially static guard poses
rejected. The provider discloses that these motions were generated with Motifect
AI. That disclosure, creator, source, license and source hashes are retained in
metadata/provenance. Personal/commercial project use is allowed; raw standalone
redistribution is prohibited. License/README accompanies the local source files.

Admission checks: source SHA-256, required arm/leg/head/root mapping, finite
normalized rotations, shared animation_quality_gate torso penetration checks,
21 sampled on-rig poses, actual limb displacement, bounded joint positions,
and retargeted-motion fingerprints within the incoming pack. Its LeftLeg/LeftShin
hierarchy required an explicit mapping convention; ordinary LeftUpLeg/LeftLeg
skeleton mappings remain unchanged. Automated checks do not guarantee that
every surviving motion will look aesthetically good or have no self-contact.
