Combat library cleanup — 2026-11-03

LATEST POLICY (supersedes the broad movement allowlist described below):
Only explicitly combat-related actions qualify. Generic walking, running,
jumping, flips, idling, acting and personality styles no longer qualify merely
because of those words. The original vocabulary remains a reference inventory;
the admission gate now requires one of 242 explicit combat terms/phrases.
Cowboy gestures and known general acting/style collections are excluded outright,
even if a title mentions shooting. An owned Store download gets no exemption.
Authored custom poses remain protected; core game movement assets remain on disk.

Additional removals: 4,051 of the previous 4,770 community entries.
Current active community catalog: 719. Sandbox currently exposes 825 records
including authored poses and built-in combat clips. The 38 validated Motifect
combat downloads remain available. Archives for this stricter pass:

“Choose a spell…” is an unselected prompt; “Animation only” is removed.

Earlier broad-pass record:

The shared Store index and Sandbox picker now accept combat actions, defensive
actions, weapons, spell gestures, acrobatics, locomotion and combat recovery.
data/combat_animation_keywords.json contains 2,557 word-aware terms and phrases.
The generator is scripts/vendor/build_combat_vocabulary.gd. It combines curated
action names, spelling/inflection variants and directional action phrases.
Generic fragments such as “forward”, “head”, “arm” and “cross” do not qualify
on their own. Names are tokenized with camel-case support. Full source
descriptions/filenames are considered, while inherited generic tags/categories
are not sufficient evidence. Compact Store rows retain the original evidence.
