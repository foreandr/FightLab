Animation quality screening and held-cast experiment (2026-11-03)

The recording showed two separate problems: Muay Thai Combination has a native
duration of 9.9666 seconds, but Sandbox compressed it into 1.5 seconds; HDM05
cross-front/back walking steps were also incorrectly matched as punches.

Library card previews now use source duration. The combat filter excludes those
cross-step walks (29 removed from the local active manifest; original files kept).

Community import version 4 applies animation_motion_conditioning.gd in source
time. It repairs isolated rotation spikes, lightly smooths small neighboring
rotations, preserves rapid strikes and original timing, and runs the existing
rig/torso-penetration gate. Persistent joint discontinuities and invalid rotations
are rejected. Cached resources are duplicated before modification. Store/Sandbox
share the same rejection index. The audit examined 627 imports: 541 accepted,
86 rejected. This is automatic screening, not a visual guarantee or full-body
collision solver. Source files stay local and preserved.

Sandbox hold experiment: hold key 1 or the Play button, release to strike.
Profiles declare min_duration/max_duration; source_duration remains unchanged.
Until explicitly authored, limits default to 70%-160% of native duration, bounded
by the shared airborne minimum and 60-second authoring limit. One second of
holding reaches maximum. Held anticipation stays within the first 3.5% of the
pose, and key-up continues forward without skipping to a later release pose.
Sandbox emission is fixed at 10%; its editable cast grip is hidden. Authoring
uses the neutral timing reference, so a hidden old draft cannot change pricing.
Follow-through runs at least at native speed. Card previews
and Repeat remain ordinary complete playback for comparing original motion.
The experiment only adapts Sandbox input. Shared Rules author the resulting
cost/damage spec; CombatRuntime retimes the existing attempt and accounts for
canceled stamina and release payment. No separate damage/resource formulas.

Dive Kick declares requires_air; it is classified Airborne without inserting an
automatic takeoff, and the shared start/release gates reject it on the floor.

Checks: animation_conditioning_test, held_cast_preview_test,
combat_animation_filter_test, sandbox_animation_library_test,
sandbox_pairing_test, combat_store_curation_test and spell_cancel_stamina_test.

Sandbox cards show Min/Max seconds from the same duration_range helper as held
input. The compact index retains duration limits, so captions do not open full
records or load animation assets. Airborne minimums apply before displaying.

Sandbox RHS browses saved_spell_library definitions by Projectile, Beam,
Targeted, Touch and Barrier. It lists actual saved names, not built-in presets
or unsaved workspaces. Empty types stay empty. Choosing a card applies its
allocation/appearance through the shared builder, retaining name, ID and
creator/date/provenance on the cast. The authoring wizard remains in Create.

Named definitions support Projectile, Touch and Self Shield. Touch contact limbs
come from the animation binding rather than being locked into the saved spell.
Self Shield saves intrinsic strength/duration (10 physical / 1 second default).
CPUs already construct these mechanics with combat_spell_rules; these were not
previously saved assets. vendor/create_practice_spells.gd adds Physical Touch,
Fire Touch (CPU allocation 15% size / 55% speed / 30% burn), and Self Barrier to
the local named library without replacing existing creations or adding copies.

Sandbox defaults to the actual Melee Hook animation and named Physical Touch.
Shared cast_charge_rules supplies the same optional hold policy to reusable
spell bindings and authored combat specs: white Tap, green Good at 1/3 s,
blue Better at 2/3 s, gold Perfect from 1.00-1.25 s, then red Overheld. Power
rises to 2x, falls smoothly after the perfect window and bottoms at 0.15x at
2.25 s. Physical damage scales; stamina has a separate modest timing surcharge, and
takeoff stamina remains owed.
Stamina uses the animation range's midpoint reference duration so stretching a quick clip cannot
accidentally change the baseline cost. Animation duration still
stays in the card's min/max range, and release continues forward from wind-up.
A fading actor-owned label quotes actual physical damage and stamina costs.
The shared transparent shield now stands 8.5 cm off the mesh, with a faint rim
and 2.5% face alpha. Charge color is a separate shell contribution, so fading
charge neither recolors nor cancels the actual Self Barrier defense.

Held Touch physical damage is calibrated to the one-second neutral benchmark:
a stationary unblocked arm tap starts at 10 physical, independent of how short
the source clip is; perfect charge scales that baseline to 20 before movement,
headshots and defenses. Damage calibration does not change mana rules.
Sandbox 2 is an always-equipped physical Left Cross shortcut (10 damage), using
the calibrated mirrored right-cross asset and shared held-cast/contact path.
It does not change the current animation/spell pair or add a hotbar button.
T remains the target-behavior keyboard shortcut; clicking the target still works.

The 2 binding now uses held_practice_shortcut over the same held_cast_preview,
cast_charge_rules and CombatRuntime path as 1. It snapshots/restores the selected
animation and spell, and supports tap, charge, perfect, overheld and interruption.
No separate strike/cost system or hotbar button is used for 2.
Stamina reference: midpoint of the source's min/max duration, independent of the
chosen hold duration. Partial charge adds up to 15%; perfect timing removes that
surcharge (1x baseline). Red overhold tapers to 0.75x baseline. Damage still uses
its separate 1x-2x-0.15x curve. Jump takeoff costs remain owed. Mana is not multiplied
by a charge tier: existing delivery, effect-share transfer and timing pricing apply.
