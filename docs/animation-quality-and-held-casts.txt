Animation quality screening and held-cast experiment (2026-11-03)

The recording showed two separate problems: Muay Thai Combination has a native
duration of 9.9666 seconds, but Sandbox compressed it into 1.5 seconds; HDM05
cross-front/back walking steps were also incorrectly matched as punches.

Library card previews now use source duration. The combat filter excludes those
cross-step walks (29 removed from the local active manifest; original files kept).

Community import version 4 applies animation_motion_conditioning.gd in source
time. It repairs isolated rotation spikes, lightly smooths small neighboring
rotations, preserves rapid strikes and original timing, and runs the existing
rig/torso-penetration gate. Persistent joint discontinuities and invalid rotations
are rejected. Cached resources are duplicated before modification. Store/Sandbox
share the same rejection index. The audit examined 627 imports: 541 accepted,
86 rejected. This is automatic screening, not a visual guarantee or full-body
collision solver. Source files stay local and preserved.

Sandbox hold experiment: hold key 1 or the Play button, release to strike.
Profiles declare min_duration/max_duration; source_duration remains unchanged.
Until explicitly authored, limits default to 70%-160% of native duration, bounded
by the shared airborne minimum and 60-second authoring limit. One second of
holding reaches maximum. Pose anticipation approaches the selected release
point while held; follow-through runs at least at native speed. Card previews
and Repeat remain ordinary complete playback for comparing original motion.
The experiment only adapts Sandbox input. Shared Rules author the resulting
cost/damage spec; CombatRuntime retimes the existing attempt and accounts for
canceled stamina and release payment. No separate damage/resource formulas.

Dive Kick declares requires_air; it is classified Airborne without inserting an
automatic takeoff, and the shared start/release gates reject it on the floor.
