Wardrobe visual review and preview performance — 2026-11-04

Creator selection
100 downloaded additions selected after inspecting front, side and rear renders
on the actual shared character: 70 headwear, 20 masks/eyewear, 9 back accessories,
and 1 belt accessory. Both masculine and feminine default bodies were rendered.
The existing fitted villager clothes and 40 hairstyles remain available.

This is NOT 100 approved items per body part. Shirts, trousers, gloves and boots
in the tested archive frequently intersect skin, float, include unrelated props,
or have incompatible bind poses. None was approved to pad those categories.
91 experimental converted modular parts also failed visual review and were not
added. Reaching roughly 100 good options per slot still requires more properly
fitted, distinct garments. Fit is reviewed at normal proportions/size; arbitrary
combinations, extreme resizing and every possible combat pose are not certified.

Preservation and provenance
All 23,460 raw catalog records and their downloaded files are preserved locally.
Unreviewed assets remain available to the developer Pavlov archive and existing
stable saved IDs still resolve. No user rejection decisions were changed.
The small reviewed manifest controls the creator only. It does not automatically
approve assets for random CPUs. Source URLs, authors and licenses remain on each
entry in assets/characters/wardrobe/curated/catalog.json. Preview images are
rendered derivatives of those listed sources and carry the same attribution.
The original large download library remains local rather than entering Git.

Performance changes
Visible reviewed cards use prepared PNGs instead of building six extra avatars.
A bounded eight-rig preview cache reuses completed appearance configurations.
Body visibility meshes and resized outfit meshes are shared with bounded caches.
Animation libraries have separate mixer ownership but reuse immutable clips;
deep-copying the whole library on every clothes change has been removed.
The shared gear-dissolve shader uses a cheaper hash and slightly larger cells.
Creator and gear edit use this same transition and preview path. Combat unchanged.

Measurements on this machine
Original 43-item review: warm repeat build median 170.6 ms, p95 233.6 ms.
After caching, 508-item review: repeat median 1.84 ms, p95 3.06 ms.
Later 111-item body review: repeat median 2.00 ms, p95 2.67 ms.
Animation-library copy alone: deep copy 46–63 ms; shallow copy 0.16–2.26 ms.
Cold first selections still load/fit models: later sample median 82.9 ms,
p95 151.4 ms, excluding initial engine/body warmup. These are construction
timings, not a claim of equivalent improvement in total frame rate.
